Notes for this song (kept in .annotator/state.json, not in the TextGrid).

Tiers

words: every sung word, in the order sung, spelled as sung. Devanagari for Hindi, Latin for English. Repeats and half lines are written as sung.

syllables: boundaries at syllable onsets. Fix the text only where the word changed (auto-filled when you change a word). ~ = held continuation of the previous syllable.

vocables: every aakar / humming stretch that is not a word: आ ओ ई ए ऊ म्म (the vowel heard), or mel for a long vowel continuing the previous syllable.

singers: A, B, C… (same letter = same person in this song), AB = two or more voices at once, empty = nobody.

When unsure

Leave the prediction as it is and mark the word uncertain with U (adds ?). Precision matters more than speed.

Whole stretch is early / late?

Drag over the stretch (or click the first word, Shift+click the last), then use Shift block below the editor, the [ ] keys (10 ms, with Shift 50 ms), or Ctrl+drag inside the selection. Ctrl+drag near a selection edge stretches the block instead. Choose which tiers move with the checkboxes.

Repeated lines (chorus)

Fix the first occurrence, select it (click first word, Shift+click last), Ctrl+C, click where the repeat starts, Ctrl+V. The pasted block stays selected: nudge it with [ ], or use paste fitted to stretch it into a selected range. Lyrics text from elsewhere: select intervals, Ctrl+Shift+V.

Workflow per song (≈15–25 min)

  1. A to see everything: singer bands, red “unlabelled voice” marks.
  2. Zoom to ~8 s. Walk the words with → (each step plays). Fix text with Enter; drag boundaries; B adds one (also while playing).
  3. Adjust syllable onsets; ✨ re-syllabifies a word.
  4. Aakars: drag over the waveform, then 1–7 on the vocables tier.
  5. Singer turns: drag a range, then 1–6 on the singers tier.
  6. Clear the Checks list (fix or ignore false alarms), Save, tick Done.
