How natural does this singing sound?

This is a short listening test for our course project on synthesising Hindi film singing. Each page plays the same lyric line sung five ways, labelled A to E in a random order. Some versions are synthesised and one may be a real recording. Please rate each one for how natural it sounds as singing: the voice, the pronunciation and the melody line together.

There are 15 pages and the whole test takes about 15 minutes. Headphones help. Play each version at least once before rating it. Your answers are saved after every page, so you can stop and come back.
